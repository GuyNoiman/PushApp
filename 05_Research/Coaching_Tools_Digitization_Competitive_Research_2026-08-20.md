# Coaching Tools Digitization - Competitive Research

Date: 2026-08-20  
Status: Research input; product recommendations below are proposed, not yet approved.  
Stage: MVP candidates plus Future library expansion.  
Source set: eleven founder-supplied coaching worksheets/images, official product pages, and primary or
academic research where available.

## 1. Executive conclusion

The supplied materials should not become eleven unrelated questionnaires. They resolve into **eight useful
digital tools, one synthesis screen, and two rights-constrained concepts**:

1. Life Wheel;
2. Values Sort;
3. Passion Map;
4. Strength Evidence;
5. Direction Statement;
6. Best Possible Year;
7. Mirror Feedback;
8. Goal Clarity Check;
9. Values Summary - a result inside Values Sort, not a separate Tools tile;
10. Winner's Code six-stage method - do not digitize without ownership or a license;
11. Blob Tree / character tree - do not reproduce without a license; an original non-diagnostic scene
    check-in may be designed instead.

The strongest PushApp advantage is not digitizing paper. It is connecting insight to action, with the user's
approval:

`Tool insight -> Coach conversation -> Dream clarification -> optional Journey proposal`

Competitors are usually strong at one isolated assessment or reflection. PushApp can preserve a result,
compare it over time, let the Coach reason from it, and turn only approved insights into a Dream or Journey.
The tool must never silently rewrite an active Journey.

## 2. Evaluation criteria

Each source was evaluated against:

- whether a proven or widely used digital implementation exists;
- whether the interaction gains something from being digital;
- whether the result can improve Coach context or future action;
- whether the model is an assessment, a reflection, or merely a worksheet;
- privacy and emotional risk;
- intellectual-property constraints;
- fit with PushApp's philosophy and official terminology.

## 3. Tool-by-tool findings

### 3.1 Life Wheel

**Source:** `Wheel of Life - coaching code.pdf` - rate life domains from 1-10, identify three domains to
advance, then choose the most important one.

**Digital market:** strong and mature. Wheeloflife.io offers an interactive radial assessment across twelve
domains and turns the result into strengths, blind spots, and an action-plan starting point. LifeWheel offers
a four-minute interactive assessment, printable wheel, historical coaching context, privacy controls, and
coach sharing.

**What works:** direct manipulation, immediate visualization, a before/after snapshot, and choosing where to
focus after seeing the whole picture.

**PushApp recommendation:** build. Use 8-10 configurable domains, one large radial visualization, tap/drag
rating, then select up to three areas and one current priority. Save dated snapshots and allow comparison.
Offer `Talk to Coach about this` after the result. Do not automatically create a Dream or Journey.

References: [Wheeloflife.io](https://wheeloflife.io/),
[LifeWheel for coaches](https://lifewheel.us/for-coaches/).

### 3.2 Values Sort and Values Summary

**Sources:** `Values Bank.pdf` and `Values Summary Table.pdf`. The bank is a long unstructured list; the
summary table asks the participant to combine values surfaced by several previous exercises and notice the
3-5 recurring values.

**Digital market:** excellent reference implementations exist. My Values Card Sort digitizes the public-
domain Personal Values Card Sort as an 83-card, three-pile flow, followed by narrowing, ranking, personal
definitions, saving, resuming, and privacy protections. ValuesCardSort uses a visually oriented card flow.

**What works:** forced trade-offs. A searchable checklist is fast but produces too many "important" values.
The best products sort broadly, then narrow to ten and five, allow custom values, and ask the user what each
selected value means personally.

**PushApp recommendation:** build one tool, not two. Flow: quick orientation -> card sort into `Very
important`, `Important`, `Not for me now` -> narrow to 10 -> narrow to 5 -> order -> define each value in the
user's words -> optional `How closely am I living this?` rating. The supplied summary table becomes the
result screen, enriched by earlier PushApp tool results only after explicit user approval.

**Rights:** use a confirmed public-domain or licensed value set and write original definitions. Do not copy
the supplied branded bank verbatim merely because a PDF was provided.

References: [My Values Card Sort](https://myvaluescardsort.com/),
[ValuesCardSort](https://www.valuescardsort.com/),
[Personal Values Card Sort](https://personalvalues.net/).

### 3.3 Passion Map

**Sources:** `Passion Identification Exercise.pdf` and the first part of `Creating a Direction
Statement.pdf`. The exercise generates 10-20 loved activities and repeatedly asks why, to expose underlying
motivators.

**Digital market:** Ikigai tools commonly ask guided questions about what the user loves, is good at, the
world needs, and can support economically. Many then generate an AI purpose statement and Venn diagram.
This is adjacent, but most products jump too quickly from answers to an authoritative-sounding result.

**PushApp recommendation:** build as reflection, not a test. Present one prompt at a time; accept text or
voice; turn answers into editable activity cards; ask `What do you love about this?`; let the user group cards
into themes; then let the Coach suggest patterns clearly labelled as suggestions. Preserve the raw answers
privately and require confirmation before any theme becomes a saved insight.

References: [Ikigai Tool](https://www.ikigaitool.com/ikigai-tool),
[Ikigai app](https://ikigaiapp.co/?lang=en).

### 3.4 Strength Evidence

**Sources:** `Talent Identification Exercise.pdf` and the skills portion of `Creating a Direction
Statement.pdf`. The paper asks for 30 skills or talents using life-history prompts.

**Digital market:** validated assessment products dominate. Gallup CliftonStrengths identifies 34 talent
themes through timed paired statements. VIA measures 24 character strengths and publishes research-use
instruments and psychometric information. HIGH5 combines a strengths assessment with application guidance,
peer feedback, stories, and 360 feedback.

**What works:** useful products do not stop at a flattering label; they connect a strength to evidence and
how to apply it.

**PushApp recommendation:** do not invent a pseudo-validated score. Build an evidence-led reflection: short
prompts from childhood, work, relationships, and recurring requests for help -> one example per claimed
strength -> user groups evidence into candidate strengths -> Coach proposes editable labels -> user chooses
five and describes when each helps. A future licensed validated assessment may be integrated separately.

References: [Gallup CliftonStrengths overview](https://support.gallup.com/hc/en-us/articles/44814767818643-What-is-the-CliftonStrengths-assessment),
[VIA assessments](https://www.viacharacter.org/researchers/assessments),
[HIGH5 features](https://high5test.com/features/).

### 3.5 Direction Statement

**Source:** `Creating a Direction Statement.pdf`. It intersects passions and core abilities, drafts five
statements, selects the most energizing one, reduces it to eight words, and defines every word.

**Digital market:** Ikigai and purpose tools provide the closest interaction reference. Their common
weakness is presenting a generated purpose statement as a discovery rather than a hypothesis.

**PushApp recommendation:** build only after Passion Map and Strength Evidence, while still allowing manual
input. Use two editable trays (`What draws me` and `What I bring`), a sentence composer, five saved variants,
an excitement rating, a word-count refinement step, and word-by-word meaning. Coach suggestions are optional
and always editable. The final statement is not a Dream; it is durable Coach context that may help formulate
multiple Dreams.

Reference: [Ikigai Tool flow](https://www.ikigaitool.com/ikigai-tool).

### 3.6 Best Possible Year

**Source:** `Winning Image.pdf`. It identifies three goals, reasons, strengths, anticipated barriers and
supporters, then asks for a one-page letter written from one year in the future in present-tense gratitude,
covering personal life, career, relationships, and an ordinary day.

**Digital market and evidence:** future-letter apps offer scheduled delivery, archives, photos, voice, and
private local storage. The research analogue is the Best Possible Self intervention. A meta-analysis of 29
studies with 2,909 participants found improvements in wellbeing, optimism, and positive affect compared with
controls. Online writing and imagery variants have both shown benefit.

**PushApp recommendation:** build, but rename generically rather than copy branded wording. Flow: choose a
future date -> short grounding exercise -> prompts across life areas -> write or dictate a future letter ->
optional guided imagery/audio playback -> schedule a private return date -> extract candidate aspirations
for review. Nothing becomes a Dream until the user approves it in a Coach conversation.

References: [Future Yourself](https://futureyourself.app/index.html),
[Best Possible Self meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6756746/),
[online BPS format comparison](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005791623000046).

### 3.7 Mirror Feedback

**Source:** `Mirror Exercise.pdf`. A respondent states their relationship to the user, then names three
helpful and three limiting qualities.

**Digital market:** the closest mature product is the licensed Reflected Best Self Exercise from the
University of Michigan. It invites significant people to provide stories, tracks responses, compiles a best-
self portrait, and produces an action plan. HIGH5 also supports peer feedback and stories.

**PushApp recommendation:** potentially valuable, but sensitive and social. Create an original flow, not a
copy of RBSE: user selects 3-5 trusted contributors, previews the exact request, sets whether responses are
identified, and can close collection early. Ask for behavior and examples, not fixed personality judgments:
`When have you seen me at my best?`, `What strength was I using?`, and `What could help me use it more?` Avoid
the blunt `three weaknesses` formulation. Results remain private by default, with wellbeing copy and block/
report controls for abusive responses.

**Stage:** Future unless the social, moderation, consent, notification, deletion, and privacy requirements
are deliberately brought into scope.

References: [University of Michigan RBSE](https://positiveorgs.bus.umich.edu/cpo-tools/rbse/),
[HIGH5 peer feedback](https://high5test.com/features/).

### 3.8 Goal Clarity Check (SMART)

**Source:** supplied SMART image: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

**Digital market:** SMART is commonly embedded inside goal-setting products rather than offered as a lasting
standalone destination. Goals: AI Coach combines SMART with conversational goal creation and converts goals
into steps, reminders and progress. Research also supports education plus follow-up, rather than a one-time
worksheet, for improving SMART goal-writing behavior.

**PushApp recommendation:** do not create a parallel Journey builder. The tool accepts an existing aspiration
or draft goal, reviews five dimensions conversationally, shows a clear before/after comparison, and offers a
revised formulation. The user may then take the result to the Coach. Only the Coach, with confirmation, may
create or update the Dream relationship or propose a future Journey; an active Journey is not silently
changed.

References: [Goals: AI Coach](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/goals-ai-coach-habit-tracker/id1474850893),
[goal-writing RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20367810/).

### 3.9 Winner's Code six-stage method

**Source:** supplied branded six-stage image. It describes a larger proprietary coaching process: awareness,
rules and barriers, winning image, environment, transformation, and reinforcements.

**Assessment:** this is not a small Tool. It is closer to a creator-authored Journey or workshop and overlaps
the future Creator Journey Authoring Platform. The supplied image does not establish PushApp's right to copy
the branded method, language, or sequence.

**Recommendation:** do not design or implement a faithful digital version until the founder confirms
ownership or obtains permission. If rights exist, model it as a multi-Milestone Journey with media, Coach
instructions, reflection inputs, and completion rules - not as a Tools questionnaire.

### 3.10 Character Tree / Blob Tree

**Source:** supplied `Character Tree.jpg`, derived from Pip Wilson and Ian Long's Blob Tree concept.

**Digital market and rights:** Blob Tree is an active commercial communication-tool catalog. The official
site identifies Pip Wilson as creator and Ian Long as illustrator and sells individual scenes and themed
collections. Public copies visibly carry copyright notices.

**Recommendation:** do not reproduce, trace, recolor, label, or algorithmically interpret the supplied image
without a license. If licensed, treat the response as a conversation opener, never a psychological score.
Otherwise create an original PushApp `Where are you right now?` scene with a different setting, characters,
composition, interaction and art direction; ask the user what their choice means instead of assigning a
meaning.

References: [Blob Tree creators](https://www.blobtree.com/pages/pip),
[official Blob Tree catalog](https://www.blobtree.com/pages/blob-links).

## 4. Recommended product sequence

### MVP candidates

1. **Life Wheel** - high visual value, bounded scope, immediately useful Coach context.
2. **Values Sort** - mature interaction pattern and useful durable result.
3. **Best Possible Year** - evidence-backed and strongly aligned with Dreams.
4. **Goal Clarity Check** - useful if tightly integrated with Coach rather than duplicating Journey creation.

### Next

5. **Passion Map**.
6. **Strength Evidence**.
7. **Direction Statement** after the two inputs above.

### Future / sensitive

8. **Mirror Feedback**, after social safety and consent work.
9. **Original scene check-in**, only if sufficiently distinct from Blob Tree or licensed.
10. **Winner's Code Journey**, only with confirmed rights and creator-platform support.

## 5. Shared product rules

- Every tool states whether it is reflection, education, or assessment. Do not imply diagnosis or scientific
  validation where none exists.
- Save progress and allow exit/resume.
- Show estimated time before starting.
- One question or cognitive operation per screen.
- Let the user edit AI-generated themes and explain why a suggestion was made.
- Tool results are private by default.
- Coach access to results must follow the approved privacy model; sharing or converting an insight into a
  Dream requires explicit user confirmation.
- Preserve dated results so repeatable tools can show change without framing the old result as failure.
- Provide delete/reset per result.
- Support RTL and language-specific content from the first release.
- Do not award XP merely for opening a tool. If Tools later qualify for Achievements, eligibility must require
  meaningful completion under the separate Achievements policy.

## 6. Design references to carry forward

- Life Wheel: direct radial manipulation and dated comparison, not a long slider form.
- Values: one-card-at-a-time sorting, undo, review, narrow, rank, define.
- Passion/strengths: editable evidence cards and clustering, not a score reveal.
- Direction Statement: visual composition from confirmed chips plus five drafts.
- Best Possible Year: quiet writing ritual, optional voice and media, sealed return moment.
- Mirror Feedback: invitation status and story-based synthesis, not a public rating.
- Goal Clarity Check: five-step diagnostic and before/after wording.

## 7. Open decisions

1. Confirm whether the supplied Winner's Code materials are owned/licensed for product use.
2. Decide whether to pursue a Blob Tree license or commission an original, meaningfully different scene.
3. Decide which of the four MVP candidates should be designed and implemented first.
4. Decide whether results automatically become available to the Coach or require a per-result `Share with

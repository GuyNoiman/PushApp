# PRD — Missions

Status: **Future Vision placeholder; not yet specified or approved for implementation.**
Stage: **Future** — explicitly removed from MVP by the founder on 2026-08-12.
Owner: founder + AI product team.
Related: B4, `Points_and_Leveling_PRD.md`, `Achievements_Engine_PRD.md`, and weekly friend-support activity.

---

## 1. Preserved product direction

A Mission is a game-generated activity independent of a Journey. Missions are not required for personal
progress and must not turn PushApp into a task manager or reward time in the app.

The only newly discussed candidate is the optional weekly friend-support Mission recorded in
`Points_and_Leveling_PRD.md`: up to three distinct eligible friends, immediate rising XP awards, and a reset at
the user's authoritative week boundary. That candidate remains part of the Future XP direction and does not
activate Missions in the MVP.

Founder future direction (2026-08-12): Missions may also gently encourage a user to explore PushApp and learn
what its different areas can do. These must be bounded first-run/discovery activities, not recurring rewards
for navigation, screen opens, or time-in-app. Exact discovery Missions and whether their completion earns a
non-XP acknowledgement require the future Mission specification.

The existing MissionEngine, Mission screen, translations, or sample Missions are legacy/dormant implementation
assets. They do not establish the approved future Mission catalog or reward model.

## 2. Decisions required before promotion

- the purpose of Missions within the coach-first product;
- daily versus weekly cadence and maximum frequency;
- which behaviors are meaningful enough to become Missions;
- XP, Coins, Achievement, or non-economic rewards;
- personalization, accessibility, replacement, and opt-out behavior;
- anti-gaming and social/privacy protections;
- relationship to Weekly Review and the authoritative week boundary;
- the initial Mission catalog and presentation.

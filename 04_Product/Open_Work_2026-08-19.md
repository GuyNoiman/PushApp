## 1. Buildable now, no founder input needed

### 1.1 The streak counts the wrong thing — HIS definition, not the current one
**Founder, 2026-08-19:** *"ההגדרה של streak זה מספר הימים בהם ביצעתי את כל today's steps."*

The shipped rule (`app/src/core/config/streak.ts`, recorded as D26.4) is far more forgiving: the
streak breaks **only when an URGENT Step is missed**, where urgent means the Journey has no slack
left in the week (the sessions still required are at least the days remaining). Every other miss
costs nothing, which is why his streak rose on a day he had not finished.

**What to build:** a day counts toward the streak only when EVERY Step scheduled for that day was
completed. Decide explicitly — and write down — how these interact, because each will surprise
somebody:
- a day with **no** scheduled Steps (neutral, or does it break?);
- a Step reported **Partial**;
- a Step reported **Couldn't** (D36 treats it as an honest report, not a failure);
- a **postponed** Step, and a **dropped** one (dropped is out of scope everywhere else);
- a **frozen** Journey's Steps.

The old rule is a founder decision (D26.4), so supersede it in the Decision Log rather than
deleting it, and keep the reasoning: it was built forgiving on purpose.

### 1.2 Merge Postpone and Reschedule into one action
Founder on device: the two read as the same thing. Keep **one** action, "Postpone"; after tapping,
the user chooses a specific time or an automatic one, and may add a reason. Today they are two rows
in the report sheet with overlapping meaning (`components/journey/RecoveryFlow.tsx`).

### 1.3 The shared completion image loses the whole design
**The most visible of these — it is what the partner will see.** The completion card is designed
and looks right on screen; tapping Share sends plain text ("You did it / Build core strength") with
none of it. It must share as an IMAGE identical to what was shown. Needs a view-to-image capture.
Check first whether that is possible without a new native dependency; if it is not, say so and let
the founder decide before adding one.

### 1.4 Options for a completed card's background
He likes the current completed-Step design and wants to consider a more prominent background so a
done card stands out. **Render the options for him to look at** — he has said repeatedly that he
wants to see, not read descriptions.

### 1.5 Home's scroll-to-top is still unverified
Fixed in a previous session, never seen working on a device. Not a code task — ask him to
double-tap the Home tab and say whether it scrolls up.

# PRD — Support Score

Status: **Future Vision** — concept confirmed; not approved for implementation.
Stage: **Future**.
Owner: founder + AI product team.
Related: B1 Points/Leveling, B3 Achievements, `../Friend_Profile_PRD.md`, and
`../Journey_Support_Circle_PRD.md`.

---

## 1. Purpose

Support Score may represent how meaningfully a user supports friends across their Journeys. It belongs
to the future Points and Leveling reframe, not to the Friend Profile MVP.

## 2. Approved direction

- The score may eventually appear on a Friend Profile.
- It is calculated from real support behavior rather than time in app or raw interaction volume.
- It must never punish a user for declining an invitation, setting a boundary, being inactive, or not
  sending enough messages/Cheers.
- It must not become a public popularity ranking or encourage performative support.
- Its formula, inputs, visibility, anti-gaming rules, and relationship to Points/Level are intentionally
  deferred until the broader Points/Leveling design is specified.

## 3. Promotion gate

Before moving this PRD out of `Future/`, define the behavioral hypothesis, score formula, qualifying
events, caps/decay, privacy model, abuse resistance, user explanation, appeal/correction behavior, and
product-guardian review. No implementation should infer a score from existing social-event counts.

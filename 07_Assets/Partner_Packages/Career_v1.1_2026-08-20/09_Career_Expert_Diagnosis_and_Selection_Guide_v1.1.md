# Career Expert — Diagnosis & Journey Selection Guide v1.1

## 1. The rule from Guy that this package now follows

**Different Milestones = different Journeys.**  
**Same Milestones with different pace/workload/path = versions of the same Journey.**

The current Career library contains **60 Journeys** and **0 authored Journey Versions**.

In every current multi-Journey Goal Family, the member Journeys have different Milestone arcs. The axis is still important: it explains **how the Journeys differ and what question places the user among them**.

---

## 2. Selection order

1. Understand the user's goal in their own words.
2. Diagnose subtype and primary bottleneck.
3. Route to the correct Goal Family.
4. If the family has several Journeys, ask/use the family's axis question.
5. Use onboarding/profile data as a weaker secondary pull.
6. Stop asking once the routing decision is sufficiently supported.

The profile does **not** decide whether the user's professional problem is target, proof, access, capability, or another bottleneck. That is the Career Expert's job.

---

## 3. Exact example Guy asked for

User says:

> “אני מגיש מועמדות ולא חוזרים אליי.”

Do **not** immediately rewrite the CV or tell them to network.

### Question 1 — Target

> לאילו תפקידים אתה מגיש כרגע — משפחה אחת די ברורה, או כמה סוגי תפקידים שונים?

If the user is applying broadly across unrelated roles:
→ `DIRECTION_GAP`  
→ `CAR_G08`

If the target is reasonably clear:
→ continue.

### Question 2 — Capability vs proof

> כשאתה מסתכל על 2–3 דרישות מרכזיות של תפקיד היעד, יש לך דוגמאות אמיתיות שמראות שכבר עשית משהו דומה?

If they genuinely cannot yet do the core work:
→ `CAPABILITY_GAP`  
→ route to the relevant skill/transition family.

If they can do it but cannot make the experience visible/credible:
→ `PROOF_GAP`  
→ `CAR_G09`

If they have credible examples:
→ continue.

### Question 3 — Access

> איך אתה מגיע לרוב ההזדמנויות — כמעט רק דרך הגשות פתוחות, או שיש גם שיחות, חיבורים ואנשים שמכירים אותך?

If the user relies almost entirely on anonymous applications and rarely reaches relevant people:
→ `OPPORTUNITY_ACCESS_GAP`  
→ `CAR_G10`

If access is reasonable:
→ continue.

### Question 4 — Process / evidence volume

> כמה חיפוש ממוקד כבר עשית עם יעד ברור, הוכחות רלוונטיות וגישה סבירה — והיכן בדיוק התהליך נשבר?

- Tiny sample → do not over-diagnose yet.
- Search repeatedly collapses because the process is unsustainable → inspect `CAR_G11`.
- User reaches interviews but stalls → `CAR_G12`.
- No clear pattern despite credible target/proof/access → keep diagnosing; do not force a Journey.

---

## 4. Why this order matters

A user can say “nobody answers” for very different reasons:

- the target is too broad;
- they can do the work but do not show it;
- they have proof but no access;
- the search process itself is unstable;
- there is not enough evidence yet to know.

The coach should not motivate past an unresolved diagnosis.

---

## 5. Goal-Family axis vs onboarding

Once the Expert has selected the family, the family axis becomes the main within-family placement tool.

Example: `CAR_G11 — sustainable job search`

Axis question:

> איזו צורת חיפוש הכי סביר שתצליח להחזיק לאורך זמן — תהליך מאוד מסודר, קצב שבועי פשוט, או מינימום גמיש שמתאים גם לשבוע עמוס?

The user's onboarding profile can then provide a weaker pull:
- high structure preference;
- variable capacity;
- one-miss-derails abandonment pattern.

That is a matching decision. It is **not** the professional diagnosis.

---

## 6. Persona rule

Personas are only a test instrument.

They help us ask:

> “Would this Journey actually make sense for a believable person?”

They do not enter the app library.

A library Step must say:
- “the first option” / “the second option”;
- “the direction you are testing”;
- “the target role”;

not:
- a synthetic person's role;
- their Dream;
- their personal reasons.

---

## 7. Form of address

Author in the form that is natural. The app already converts to the user's chosen form of address. This package does not duplicate every Step into masculine/feminine variants.

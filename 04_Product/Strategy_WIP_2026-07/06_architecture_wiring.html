① Structural wiring — the layers & their boundaries

Engines never import each other; they emit and react over the bus. Everything below the dashed line is reachable only through a gateway interface (with a Null fallback + feature flag) — so the app runs at $0, fully offline, with every backend switched off.

③ If you build one thing first

Two nodes carry disproportionate leverage. Everything adaptive hangs off them.

★StepMissed

Nothing emits it today. The moment the Scheduler does, it unlocks — in order — Miss-Recovery → Stall alerts → Intervention → Profiling → Motivation. It's a cheap change with the widest blast radius in the whole plan.

★Analytics

You didn't list it, but it's the hidden root of every "learning" feature. Profiling, Motivation, adaptive comms and recommendations all drink from it. Design its privacy-safe signal schema early, or each loop invents its own collection — a privacy landmine.

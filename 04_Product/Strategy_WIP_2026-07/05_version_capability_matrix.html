How to read the shape

  • V1 is almost entirely green — the loop is built. V1's only new piece is Categories.
  • V2 is where "ignition" lands: onboarding, first-party library, light + Level-1 AI, miss-recovery, Grace, i18n.
  • V3 turns on money and depth: subscription, adaptive intervention, authoring, real-life accountability, weekly-AI.
  • V4 is the platform bet: open marketplace, creator economy, rich media, competition, moderation, JITAI — the highest-risk column.
  • Future holds what we architect for but don't ship until proven (unbounded free AI chat).

Step Dependency Cards — waiting stack (Rev 2)

REV 2 — revised per founder feedback + reference image

Stage: MVP (design-only render, per Step_Dependencies_PRD.md — no code changes). Visual answer to §2.3 / §4h / §4j of that PRD: how a locked-behind Step is shown, and how it reads in RTL. Rendered against the current mature app tokens (theme.ts, 2026-08-07 redesign — single teal accent, amber reserved for urgency, Inter throughout).

  1. Deck of equal cards, not slivers. Rev 1 showed the hidden Steps as thin receding edges. Per the founder's reference, hidden cards are now the exact same width and height as the top card — a real stacked deck, sitting directly behind the card being worked on, not off to the side.
  2. Waiting, not blocked. Still no padlock, no grey/disabled treatment. The hidden cards stay in the same white-card family as an ordinary Step card — they're just behind, with a blank face (no title, no content leaks through). There's nothing to tap back there, so nothing is drawn as tappable-then-refused.
  3. Depth is literal. One full card-outline peeking behind = one Step waiting. The founder's "up to 3" chain becomes a front card plus up to 2 identical outlines behind it. When the predecessor is reported, the chain shortens by one: the next card promotes to the full front position and the remaining hidden card moves up to take its place.
  4. Offset & RTL. Each hidden layer is nudged ~10px down and ~10px toward the trailing edge per layer (2nd layer = 20px, so its edge is still legible past the 1st). The horizontal nudge is driven by a --stack-dir token that's 1 in LTR and flips to -1 under dir="rtl", so the whole deck mirrors to the opposite edge for free — see the Hebrew demo below.
  5. Hint copy. Sits below the stack (not crammed into a card), so a long predecessor title can wrap. Recommended option marked below.

Miss-Recovery Flow

A missed Step funnels through closed lists; only “Other” opens AI. Each reason maps to a system lever — most by rule, not AI.

Step missed → Postpone or Cancel · −1 Grace Token → “What happened?” (closed list)

Only the last option — “Other” — opens the AI chat. Everything above it is $0 and fully scripted.

Each reason drives a lever — most by rule, not AI
Forgot→Change reminder timing / frequencyrule
No time→Reschedule / reduce the week’s loadrule
Too hard→Resize the Step / edit Journey rulesrule
Lost motivation→Reconnect to the Dream · boost motivation · refer an Allyrule
Life got in the way→Grace — no change, self-compassionrule
Other (free text) → AI: respond with empathy and classify into one lever above (or a new one) AI

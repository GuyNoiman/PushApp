# Career Diagnosis — Approved Card Copy v1.0
The card is a fallback/shortcut under an open conversational question. The wording should help the user recognize their situation without teaching them which route is 'better'.
**Important:** the key shown here is the actual contract `value`. There is no second answer-kind identifier.

## `careerDirectionClarity`
**Question:** How clear are you about the direction or kind of role you are aiming for right now?
- `unclear` — **EN:** I’m still figuring out what direction fits me  
  **HE:** אני עדיין מנסה להבין לאן נכון לי לכוון
- `partial` — **EN:** I have a couple of directions I’m considering  
  **HE:** יש לי כמה כיוונים על הפרק
- `clear` — **EN:** I have a fairly clear direction in mind  
  **HE:** יש לי כיוון די ברור

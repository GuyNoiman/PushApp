Cross-cutting — true of every box above

§Privacy & data-minimisation. RLS everywhere, PII quarantined, consent + account deletion + data export. Every new domain routes through security-privacy first.
§Config-before-code. Categories, templates, rewards, tiers, missions all live in registries, not hard-coded logic.
§Store compliance. UGC + AI + social all change the App Privacy label and add review surface (moderation, deletion).
§Cost governance. AI usage caps, CDN bandwidth, push volume — each metered before it ships (CLAUDE.md §3.10).
§Localisation. Repo is English; users are Israeli — a he/en layer becomes real once content is user-facing at scale.
§Onboarding / first 30 seconds. The "ignition" moment that makes a stuck user feel understood — cuts across Auth, AI, Journey.

## 1. What this is, and what it is not

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} is a personal-growth coach. It helps you turn something you want to change about
yourself into a Journey — a finite plan made of Steps, and sometimes Milestones — and it adapts that
plan as you go, using an AI-driven coach that talks with you and reads what you report.

It is **not therapy, not medical advice, not psychological treatment, and not a crisis service.**
Nothing the coach says is a diagnosis, a prescription, or a professional opinion, and nothing here
should be treated as one, however specific or confident it sounds.

For a small number of subjects, the coach does not build a plan at all. **Today that means goals
about addiction and about a relationship in serious difficulty.** When your goal falls there, the
coach says so and hands the conversation to a person instead — a friend, a professional, or a support
service — rather than turning it into a Journey. That is a deliberate limit built into the product,
not a gap we have not gotten to yet, and it may be extended to other subjects over time without
notice, because the direction of that change only ever adds caution.

**If you are in crisis, thinking about harming yourself, or someone else is in danger, {{PRODUCT_NAME}}
is not the right place — please contact your local emergency number, or a crisis line where you are.**
For example, in the United States that is 988; in the United Kingdom and Ireland, Samaritans can be
reached at 116 123. These are examples, not an exhaustive or guaranteed-current list — if a number
does not work where you are, search for a crisis line in your country, or go to a hospital.

## 4. The AI coach

The coach's words are **generated by an AI model, in response to what you tell it.** That has two
consequences worth saying plainly:

- **It can be wrong.** It can misunderstand you, suggest a Step that does not fit your life, or state
  something with confidence it has not earned. Use your own judgement, especially about anything that
  affects your health, your money, your safety, or another person.
- **It is not a professional opinion.** Not medical, not psychological, not legal, not financial. The
  coach adapts a plan from what you report; it does not diagnose, treat, or advise in any of those
  senses, and nothing it says carries the weight of someone licensed to do those things.

The coach is built with limits it does not cross by design — for example, it will not give clinical or
medical directives, and it will not act on plainly dangerous requests. If it ever declines to help
with something for that reason, that is the product working as intended, not a bug to route around.

## 5. What you write, and what we may do with it

**What you write is yours.** A Journey's "why", a reflection, a note you leave when a day did not go
well — these stay yours, in every sense that matters, including that by default their raw wording
never leaves your device (the Privacy Policy §3 explains exactly what does and does not travel, in
full).

To run the service, you give us a limited licence to your content: to store it, to process it —
including sending the specific, minimised parts the Privacy Policy names to the coach or to the
Mirror synthesis service when a feature you are using genuinely requires it — and to show it back to
you and to the specific people you chose to share it with (an Ally, your Support Circle, a Mirror
contributor). That licence goes no wider than that. We do not sell what you write, we do not hand it
to advertisers, and where a feature sends part of it to an outside service, that service's terms — as
described in the Privacy Policy — do not permit training a model on it.
